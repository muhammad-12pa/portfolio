Explainable hiring and scam detection

Category: machine learning, web application

Problem

Fake job offers and hiring scams reach job seekers through listings and messages. A plain "scam or not" label gives them no reason to trust it.

Objectives

  • Classify a job post as genuine or suspicious.
  • Show why a post was flagged (LIME and SHAP, planned).
  • Offer the tool as a website anyone can open.

Solution

Models trained in a Jupyter notebook (11 sections) and saved to a model folder. A Flask app loads them and serves the pages. Separate modules handle the chatbot and the explanations. ADD: dataset name and model type
